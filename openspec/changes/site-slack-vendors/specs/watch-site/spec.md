## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Public installation page
Watch SHALL have a static page at t3-watch.lfglabs.ai with the README's 60-second recording,
the check kinds, installation requirements and commands, and links to its MCP Registry entry
and public source. It SHALL identify Watch as free and MIT-licensed and include a favicon,
an Open Graph image and security.txt with contact@lfglabs.ai.

#### Scenario: A visitor installs Watch
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the site on a phone or desktop
- **THEN** they can play the demo, read what Watch checks and copy the installation commands
- **AND** they can reach the registry and source without signing in
