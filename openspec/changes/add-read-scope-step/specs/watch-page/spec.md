## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: What Watch may read

Before the first scan and before each Add services, the page SHALL show the folders and logins
Watch would read, each as a tick, all ticked to begin with or as last saved, and SHALL let the
person add a folder. Scan these SHALL save the choice and then scan. The chat view SHALL show the
choice without ticks it can change, with a button to the page.

#### Scenario: First setup

- **WHEN** a person opens a new Watch
- **THEN** the page lists the folders with their repository counts and the three logins, all
  ticked, and scans only after the person presses Scan these
